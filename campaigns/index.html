// what these are

Independent field guides

Each guide is a plain-language brief on a live issue where technology is used against communities: what it is, the topics that matter, the talking points that don't hold up, straight answers to common questions, and concrete ways to push back. Built to be read in five minutes or five sections.

Independent education. These guides are put together by scrypt-kitty from public sources only — reporting, public records, and other organizations' published work. They are not affiliated with, and do not reproduce the internal work of, any single group. Where a local coalition is doing the deep, sourced work, we link to them.
